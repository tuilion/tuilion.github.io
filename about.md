This is my personal blog where I write my thoughts and impressions of the world.
Topics range from pure philosophy, coding, academia impressions, and family moments. I use this space to talk to anyone who deals with life in general, thinks about his success, likes to build things and practises new skills. I am aware today people mostly prefer social media as a form of expression and a way to get word out to people, but I find it too shallow and prone to virality and hype.

I've spent years in academia, I hold a PhD, I skate regularly, I have two kids and I like gaming. This blog documents my "transition" from grinding an academic life and institutionalised thinking to something more down-to-earth, connected to people and life around me - but not denying the skills I acquired so far.

I believe in strong fundamentals, curiosity-driven work, and getting my hands dirty.
